About Me

Hello! My name is Femi and I enjoy building things that live on the internet. My interest is in information security and SaaS Systems. My interest started back in 2018 when I decided to try to secure my home network and understand the vulnerability exposure of the assets on the network — turns out this exploration and building automations taught me a lot about HTML, Python, & security vulnerabilties!
